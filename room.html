**Being an Influencer**

When you are posting online, you have to be very careful because certain things will make people cringe instantly. If you write super long paragraphs in your captions or use thirty different hashtags on one photo. Other than that, forget about posting tired trends, outdated audio clips, or anything that everyone else has already overdone, because copying old internet templates is an instant way to look unoriginal. Instead, lock in on top-tier current trends like photo dumps that tell a subtle story, unbothered lifestyle check-ins, or hyper-specific relatable humor or just follow up trends. Keep your grid clean and your vibes authentic.

**Taste in Music**

If you belong to Gen X or the millennial crew, I already know your playlists are filled with tired old radio hits and dated tracks. And if you happen to be Pakistani, seriously, it is time to let go of those ancient 90s classics. Clinging to that old stuff completely ruins your modern vibe and holds you back. Instead, you need to stream the freshest trends and current pop anthems that actually prove you belong in the Gen Z conversation, because staying locked into what's topping the charts right now is top-tier.

**Texting Shortcuts**

Whenever you are texting your friends, you need to ditch formal spelling completely and start using quick shorthand terms. But fair warning: if you are a millennial or a Gen X trying too hard, do not overdo it, or you might look a little silly because it just does not match your usual energy (lol). The trick is to use them naturally so you save time and look effortless. Here are the essential short forms you need to start using right now:

  • **ALR:** Alright
  • **GTG:** Got to go
  • **BRB:** Be right back
  • **IDC:** I don't care
  • **IDK:** I don't know
  • **TBH:** To be honest
  • **IMO:** In my opinion
  • **RN:** Right now
