**Supplies and Accessories**

When it comes to what you bring to school, forget about boring, plain supplies and traditional backpacks, because carrying that kind of gear makes you look totally out of touch. You need to upgrade your daily aesthetic with cute, trending items like a Bright bag or cute bow pouches to hold all your pens, and fill them up with aesthetic pink pencils and pastel highlighters. Even your shoes need to be on point, meaning you have to ditch any stiff or old-fashioned footwear in favor of trendy sneakers or loafers that actually match the modern vibe. Everything you carry should look curated, cute, and completely effortless, proving you actually care about the details without trying too hard.

**How to Look Charming**

If you want to look effortlessly gorgeous, it all starts with the basics. Drink about 2000 ml of water every day to keep your skin glowing, and stick to a simple skincare routine. Next, lean into accessories to elevate your vibe—throw on a black band and a cool watch for your wrists. When it comes to shoes, trade boring footwear for trendy sneakers that instantly make any outfit look better. If you can't wear your hair completely down, pull it back while leaving a few face-framing flicks loose. Most importantly, keep your charm alive and just be confidently yourself.
