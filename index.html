**Vocabulary and Slang**

If you are still walking around using your own old-school vocabulary and regular English, let's be real—you are so chopped and no one is going to like your vibe. You need to upgrade your dictionary immediately. Here are the slangs you need to use:

  • **Aura farming:** Doing cool things on purpose to look impressive to others.
  • **Clock it:** Pointing out a true fact that everyone else is thinking.
  • **Slay:** Doing an amazing job at something or looking great.
  • **Diva:** Expressing someone as a goddess as she looking pretty.
  • **Ate:** Doing something so well that it is near perfection.
  • **Bet:** A quick and casual way to say yes or deal.
  • **Delulu:** Being a little unrealistic or dreaming big.

**Fashion Sense**

When it comes to your closet, you have to completely ditch anything fitted or structured because skinny jeans are officially out of style. You need to wear baggy everything, from oversized graphic tees and vintage hoodies that swallow you whole to extra-wide denim pants that drag slightly on the floor. The goal is to look like you just grabbed clothes three sizes too big out of a laundry basket and decided it was a runway look. If your outfit looks neat and tailored, you are completely missing the point.
