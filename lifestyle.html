**Emojis**

If you are still out here dropping all the old-school smiling emojis and basic yellow faces, you literally have zero sense of modern communication. You have to use the crying emoji for literally every single emotion whether you are laughing, crying, or just breathing. On top of that, you can throw in the skull emoji whenever something is so funny it kills you, and use the sparkles to make any basic sentence look aesthetic. Stop using ancient punctuation and adapt to the proper emoji language right now.

**Room**

When it comes to the place where you live, you cannot be living in a boring and basic space anymore. You have to completely transform your room into a cozy sanctuary with a pink and white aesthetic, complete with warm LED lighting and string lights. Cover your walls with photo collages of your favorite celebrity groups you stan alongside your own printed-out memories. Make sure you set up a clean table, a nice chair for your mirror selfies, and a bed piled high with a super cozy blanket so it looks like a soft cloud.

**Makeup**

Your makeup routine does not have to be all cakey with heavy foundation because that look is completely dead and buried. Instead, you just need to put on your sunscreen and a good moisturizer to keep your skin looking fresh and hydrated. Add a quick touch of liquid blush and a swipe of lip gloss to finish the look. You want to look effortlessly fresh-faced without any heavy layers, because if your face looks like a painted mask, you definitely won't pass the vibe check.
